## 中文

### 谁需要商业许可

你想在自己的产品或服务里使用 Writer 的源代码或它的修改版，又不打算履行 AGPL 的义务。比如：

- 把 Writer 的引擎、编辑器或桌面应用的代码放进闭源软件，并对外分发这个软件；
- 修改 Writer 后通过网络向用户提供服务（比如在线编辑文档），但不向这些用户提供修改后的完整源代码；
- 其他不方便履行 AGPL 义务的情况。

### 谁不需要

- **使用官方 Writer 应用的所有人。** Auspex 发布的 Writer 应用对个人、企业和其他组织都免费，在工作中使用也一样，不需要任何许可。
- **个人使用。** 自己学习、研究、修改源代码，只要不对外分发，也不让别人通过网络使用你的修改版。
- **遵守 AGPL 的使用。** 你的用法满足 AGPL-3.0 和 NOTICE 附加条款的全部要求，比如对外分发或通过网络提供修改版时，向用户提供完整的对应源代码，并保留署名和版权声明。

拿不准属于哪一种，可以写信来问。

### 如何申请

发邮件到 [mosheng9@outlook.com](mailto:mosheng9@outlook.com)，写明：

- 公司或个人的名称，以及联系人；
- 打算怎样使用 Writer：用到哪些部分（引擎、编辑器、桌面应用），做成什么产品或服务；
- 分发或提供服务的方式，以及大致的规模。

我们会根据你的情况回复。商业许可的范围、期限和费用按具体情况商定，以双方签署的书面协议为准。

几点说明：

- 商业许可只涉及 Auspex 享有权利的代码。Writer 用到的第三方组件仍按各自的许可证使用，见 [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)。

## English

Writer's source code is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE), with the additional terms in [NOTICE](NOTICE). If
the AGPL's requirements do not fit what you want to do, you can ask Auspex for a commercial license.

### Who needs a commercial license

You want to use Writer's source code, or a modified version of it, in your own product or service without meeting the
obligations of the AGPL. For example:

- you put code from Writer's engine, editors or desktop app into closed-source software that you distribute;
- you modify Writer and offer it to users over a network (an online document editor, say) without giving those users
  the complete source code of your modified version;
- any other case where the AGPL's obligations do not suit you.

### Who does not

- **Anyone using the official Writer app.** The Writer app published by Auspex is free for individuals, businesses and
  other organizations, including for work. No license is needed.
- **Personal use.** Learning from, experimenting with and modifying the source code for yourself, as long as you do not
  distribute it or let others use your modified version over a network.
- **Use that complies with the AGPL.** Your use meets every requirement of the AGPL-3.0 and of the additional terms in
  NOTICE: for example, when you distribute a modified version or offer it over a network, you give its users the
  complete corresponding source code and keep the attribution and copyright notices.

If you are not sure which case is yours, write and ask.

### How to ask

Email [mosheng9@outlook.com](mailto:mosheng9@outlook.com) with:

- the name of your company (or your own name) and a contact person;
- how you plan to use Writer: which parts (the engine, the editors, the desktop app) and in what product or service;
- how it will be distributed or offered, and roughly at what scale.

We will get back to you. The scope, term and fees of a commercial license depend on the case and are set only by a
written agreement signed by both parties.

Also note:

- A commercial license covers only the code that Auspex holds the rights to. Third-party components that Writer uses
  stay under their own licenses; see [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
- A commercial license does not by itself include the right to use the Writer name or logo. If you need that, say so in
  your email.
